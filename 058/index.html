Where dev stands

dev has had no merge since 2026-11-06, thirteen days, when hazelgrove/hazel#2605 (a let's definition sees the outer binding) went in at 782249bbd0. Of our 48 open PRs, 14 could merge today with only an approval, and one of them, #2613, already has it. The last merge-a-thon, on 2026-10-29, merged 20 PRs into dev in a day.

How a PR reaches dev now. dev takes PRs through GitHub's merge queue, which builds up to five at a time, together, on top of dev, and merges them only if all are green. Two checks are required, Fast checks and Static checks, and one approving review from a code owner: every file's owner is the hazel-core team (Cyrus Omar, Alexander Bandukwala, Andrew Blinn, Thomas Porter, Matt Keenan and Matthew Hammer), and an author cannot approve their own PR. A PR's CI takes about 17 to 21 minutes (hazelgrove/hazel#2828's last two runs). Because the queue tests each group on the latest dev, a PR that is behind dev but merges cleanly needs no update first; one that conflicts must be resolved by hand.

The candidates, by size. Of the 215 open PRs on hazelgrove/hazel, 48 are ours. Nine are livelits work and left out here (#2536, #2597, #2616, #2635, #2650, #2806, #2810, #2817, #2819). The other 39 are below; the candidates of the first two groups are drawn here, by the lines each changes.
